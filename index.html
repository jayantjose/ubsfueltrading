WHAT we do

UBS FUEL TRADING LLC is bunker supplier in UAE.

The core of our business is supplying petroleum products to the marine industry and commercial outlets.

UBS FUEL TRADING LLC is well known bunker supplier at all major and minor ports in United Arab Emirates. We can supply bulk deliveries of different kinds of Marine Fuels throughout U.A.E. via Our fleet of vehicles.

UBS FUEL TRADING LLC follows strict compliance in ensuring health and safety with high consideration of the existing requirements in bunkering procedures and oil distribution processes.

UBS FUEL TRADING LLC respects the UAE law and the international standards.

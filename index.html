What we do

A trusted bunker supplier across the UAE

UBS Fuel Trading L.L.C. is a fuel and bunker supplier in the UAE. The core of our business is supplying petroleum products to the marine industry and to commercial customers.

Alongside marine fuels, we are a bulk diesel supplier for industrial and commercial customers in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman and the northern emirates, delivering Ultra Low Sulphur Diesel by road tanker.

We are a well-known supplier at all major and minor ports in the United Arab Emirates, delivering bulk quantities of every kind of marine fuel through our own fleet of road tankers.

We follow strict health and safety compliance in every bunkering and oil distribution procedure, and we respect UAE law and international standards.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our marine fuels, delivery areas and bunkering service.
Ask us directly

Who is UBS Fuel Trading L.L.C.?

UBS Fuel Trading L.L.C. is a fuel and bunker supplier in the UAE, headquartered in Dubai with branch offices in Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and Ajman. We supply marine fuels, diesel and petroleum products to the marine industry and commercial customers, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Why choose UBS Fuel Trading as your fuel supplier in the UAE?

UBS Fuel Trading delivers with its own fleet of 28 road tankers and keeps a 1,300 MT storage facility in Ajman, so supply is not dependent on third parties. We supply 25 ports and jetties in six emirates, run a 24/7 bunkering and emergency service, supply fuels to ISO 8217 specifications, and offer competitive bulk prices with flexible payment terms.

Do you supply diesel in bulk to companies in Dubai and the UAE?

Yes. We supply Ultra Low Sulphur Diesel (ULSD) and Marine Gas Oil in bulk for marine, industrial and commercial use, from 5 MT to 50 MT per delivery by road tanker, across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah and Umm Al Quwain.

Where are UBS Fuel Trading's offices?

Our head office is at Office 450, Al Qusais Industrial Area 2, Dubai. We also have branch offices in Musaffah (Abu Dhabi), Hamriyah Free Zone (Sharjah) and Al Jurf 2 (Ajman), where we run our fuel storage facility.

Which marine fuels do you supply?

We supply Marine Gas Oil (MGO/DMA), Low Sulphur Marine Gas Oil with a maximum sulphur content of 0.1% or 0.05%, Ultra Low Sulphur Diesel, Intermediate Fuel Oil (IFO 30 and IFO 180), Heavy Fuel Oil (HFO 380) and all types of blended fuel oils.

Which UAE ports do you deliver to?

We deliver to 25 ports and jetties across six emirates: Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah and Umm Al Quwain, including Jebel Ali, Port Rashid, Dubai Maritime City, Khalifa Port, Mina Zayed, Port Khalid, Khorfakkan Port and Mina Saqr.

Do you offer 24/7 and emergency bunker deliveries?

Yes. Our bunkering service runs 24/7, and out-of-hours and emergency deliveries are available. Call our 24-hour line on +971 55 127 8903.

What quantities can you deliver?

We deliver from 5 metric tonnes (MT) up to 50 MT per delivery, by road tanker, to ports, jetties and commercial sites across the UAE.

How is the fuel delivered?

Every grade is delivered by our own fleet of 28 road tankers, each with a capacity of 13,500 gallons. Our 1,300 MT storage facility in Ajman helps us keep supply uninterrupted.

How do I request a bunker quote?

Send us your vessel or site, port, fuel grade and quantity through the quote form on our contact page, email info@ubsfueltrading.com or call +971 4 256 2231. We reply to enquiries promptly.

Do you offer flexible payment terms?

Yes. Our size, independence and buying power let us pass bulk-purchase savings on to our customers, together with a range of flexible payment terms.

Need marine fuel delivered today?

Our bunkering team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for scheduled, out-of-hours and emergency deliveries across the UAE.

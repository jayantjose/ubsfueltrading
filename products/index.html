FAQ

Questions about our fuels

Do you supply diesel in bulk to companies in Dubai and the UAE?

Yes. We supply Ultra Low Sulphur Diesel (ULSD) and Marine Gas Oil in bulk for marine, industrial and commercial use, from 5 MT to 50 MT per delivery by road tanker, across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ajman, Ras Al Khaimah and Umm Al Quwain.

Which marine fuels do you supply?

We supply Marine Gas Oil (MGO/DMA), Low Sulphur Marine Gas Oil with a maximum sulphur content of 0.1% or 0.05%, Ultra Low Sulphur Diesel, Intermediate Fuel Oil (IFO 30 and IFO 180), Heavy Fuel Oil (HFO 380) and all types of blended fuel oils.

What quantities can you deliver?

We deliver from 5 metric tonnes (MT) up to 50 MT per delivery, by road tanker, to ports, jetties and commercial sites across the UAE.

How is the fuel delivered?

Every grade is delivered by our own fleet of 28 road tankers, each with a capacity of 13,500 gallons. Our 1,300 MT storage facility in Ajman helps us keep supply uninterrupted.

How do I request a bunker quote?

Send us your vessel or site, port, fuel grade and quantity through the quote form on our contact page, email info@ubsfueltrading.com or call +971 4 256 2231. We reply to enquiries promptly.

Need marine fuel delivered today?

Our bunkering team is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week for scheduled, out-of-hours and emergency deliveries across the UAE.
